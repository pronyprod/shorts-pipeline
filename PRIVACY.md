# Privacy policy: shorts-pipeline

`shorts-pipeline` is a private automation used only by the owner of this repository for the owner's own
YouTube channels. Nobody else can sign in to it.

- **What it accesses:** the owner's own YouTube channels, through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube
  Analytics API: the list of uploaded videos, their comments, and per-video statistics.
- **What it does:** posts one comment on each newly published video as the channel, and copies per-video
  statistics (views, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained, average view duration) into the owner's
  own private spreadsheet.
- **What it stores:** nothing beyond that spreadsheet. No data about viewers or any other person is
  collected, stored, shared or sold.
- **Access:** the owner can withdraw the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
