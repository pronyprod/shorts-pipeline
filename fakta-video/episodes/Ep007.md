## Checked and dropped / changed
- **"Hilang" / "ditemukan":** not used, as the bank warns. Locals knew it, and the line says so. "Tertutup" throughout.
- **"45 days" for Cornelius:** in no source; "two months" used.
- **1815 Raffles visit:** one timeline claims it; Wikipedia says he never visited (spoken line follows Wikipedia). Weakest fact in the script after the stone count.
- **"Over one million stones":** one general source says over 300,000. Million is the more widely cited figure, kept with "lebih dari".
- **"40 km from Yogyakarta":** this is a road distance (straight line is 26 km), so no number is used.
- **Two million blocks, no cement:** tourism sites only, no primary source, cut.
- **Merapi eruption of 1006 as the cause:** disputed, cut. "Abu gunung api" stays general.
- **Restoration cost and worker count:** sources conflict (US$6.9M vs ~$25M; 600 vs 800+), cut.
- **1985 attack on the stupas:** not used (TTS safety rule and off-topic).
- **Temple drawing:** our own schematic. Base and height use published sizes; the round terraces are drawn by eye, and no number is printed from them.
