## Checked and dropped / changed
- **Island list:** the bank said "Nusa Kode"; Wikipedia's current list says Gili Dasami. Used Wikipedia's names. Flores: dragons live only on its west and north coasts; the script just says "Flores".
- **Island pins:** Komodo, Rinca, Flores and Padar are placed from the map; the two gilis are approximate (small islets off Rinca/Flores, not in Natural Earth).
- **Population count:** 3,222 (2013) vs a park list summing to ~5,200: sources disagree, so no count is spoken.
- **Running speed 20 km/h, venom, 166 kg captive record, lifespan:** true but cut for length.
- **"Endangered" year:** the IUCN page itself was blocked (403); the year comes from NHM and NPR coverage of the IUCN update.
- **Wording "naik jadi terancam punah":** IUCN "Endangered" is translated as "terancam punah" (Vulnerable → Endangered is a step up).
- **Payoff "rumah satu-satunya di Indonesia":** follows from the endemic island list.
