## Checked and dropped / changed
- "Loudest sound in history": dropped. Decibel estimates vary widely (172-300 dB) and no calibrated measurement exists.
- "The wave circled the Earth 3-4 times": not used as a lap count (sources say 3¼, 3½ or 4). We use what was recorded: 7 passages over Glasgow in 5 days.
- The "eastward guns" wording (police chief's report) was not in our primary source; the VO says only "dari arah timur", which is true geographically (Krakatau lies east-north-east of Rodrigues).
- Batavia gasworks gauge (172 dB claim): not used (secondary sources only).
