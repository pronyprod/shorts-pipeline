## Checked and dropped / changed
- Dropped the exact Curie temperature (sources say 103-105 C, vary): only "sedikit di atas titik didih" is said.
- "Some cookers" keep warm at ~65 C, so screen stamp says "Sekitar, banyak model". Fuzzy-logic/induction cookers work differently; the script says the simple magnet type ("magnet kecil di bawah panci"), shown generally.
- No numbers on screen beyond 100 and 65 °C; the 25 -> 100 counter is the heating curve start (room temp, illustrative) and ends at the sourced 100.
