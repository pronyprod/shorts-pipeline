## Checked and dropped / changed
- **Exact Pontianak date:** it moves between years (20 Mar 2026, 21 Mar 2027), so the line says "sekitar".
- **City dates on screen (27 Feb, 5 Mar, 5 Apr):** these are the first dates only; each city also has a second date later in the year, not shown. Sabang's own 2026 calculation gives 4 Apr vs BMKG's 5 Apr, a one-day difference; BMKG's date is used.
- **Jakarta date:** computed, not read off a BMKG table.
- **NOAA JetStream "The Shadow Knows":** was listed as a source, but could not be found when re-checked on 2026-11-09, so it was replaced with Bucknell and Windows to the Universe.
- **Eratosthenes' distance and his final figure:** sources disagree on the Syene-Alexandria distance and the length of a stadion, so neither is stated. Only the angle and the method are used.
- **"Syene had zero shadow, exactly":** kept as the classic account ("matahari tepat di atas"); Syene is not exactly on the tropic.
- **Our Tegal experiment is a calculation,** nobody physically measured it. The script says "Coba versi kita" and "Hitung".
- **Pontianak-Tegal is not perfectly north-south;** the 0.19° longitude difference is negligible and our method ignores it, as Eratosthenes' did.
- **Person and sun drawing:** our own schematic, not to scale. The two sticks are to scale (1 m = 220 px, 12 cm = 26 px).
