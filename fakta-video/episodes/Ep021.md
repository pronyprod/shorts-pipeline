## Checked and dropped / changed
- Start of the trade is debated (c.1720 Macknight; rock-art claims earlier): only "more than 100 years before 1901" is said, true under every date.
- Dropped the Flinders name and Pobassoo from the voice (Whisper); "penjelajah Inggris" spoken, Flinders' name on screen.
- Dropped rock-art/older-contact claims (debated). Sources agree on 60 vessels only as "about".
- Distance is a straight line; screen route is schematic (not a true-scale map), labelled with the km figure only.
