## Checked and dropped / changed
- **"Ditukar" (swapped):** popular shorthand, kept in the hook as a question. The treaty let each side keep what it held; the VO says exactly that ("tetap pegang… dapat Run").
- **Price:** 300× is the most defensible figure; "60,000%" (heritage page) and "1,000%+" (generated encyclopedia) were dropped. The 1/122nd sentence is tagged "citation needed" on Wikipedia, so not used. "Beli seribu, jual tiga ratus ribu" is only an illustration of 300×.
- **Run's area:** dropped; sources disagree (1.5 to 13 km²). Only the 3 km length is used.
- **End of the monopoly:** Wikipedia's Banda page says "until the mid-19th century" and the Run page dates the transfer to 1817 (a decline of supremacy). "Tamat" is a slight simplification of that decline.
- **Bandanese population:** the VO names who paid; the numbers are on screen only, because the spoken version was blocked by Gemini's safety filter.
