## Checked and dropped / changed
- **Strait width:** sources give 20-40 km for the strait as a whole, and Wallace said 15 miles (24 km). The hook uses Wikipedia's Bali-Lombok figure with "sekitar".
- **Strait depth:** 1,400 m on one site is an outlier, not used. "Minimal 250 m" fits every other source.
- **Marsupials on Lombok itself:** not claimed. Lombok's contrast is Wallace's birds; kuskus and tree-kangaroos are further east.
- **"Burung takur" = barbet:** Wallace names no species in the passage used. "Takur" as the Indonesian name comes only from Wikipedia's interlanguage label, the weakest link here.
- **Line on the map:** simplified through the deep channels.
- **Crab-eating macaques and the Sulawesi exceptions:** left out for length.
