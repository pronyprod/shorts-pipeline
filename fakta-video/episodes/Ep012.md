## Checked and dropped / changed
- The real voyage went west round the Cape of Good Hope (much longer); we say and show "jarak lurus" (straight line) only.
- Which species the 1522 skins were is disputed (lesser vs greater bird-of-paradise): not stated. The name *apoda* belongs to the greater bird-of-paradise; the line says "nama ilmiahnya apoda" for the bird shown.
- "Sekitar seratus tahun sebelumnya": the source says "middle of the seventeenth century" (≈1640–1660, i.e. ~100–118 years before 1758); on screen "Sejak ±1650-an".
- Species total varies by source (39–45); spoken "sekitar empat puluh". "27 di Pulau Papua" = the island of New Guinea (Indonesia + PNG), not only Indonesian Papua.
- Dropped: the feather-hat trade of the 1800s (hunting, depressing), Lesson 1824 first live sighting (no room), "minum embun" legend detail (time).
