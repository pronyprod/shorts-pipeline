# Privacy policy: Prony Production shorts poster

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Prony Production runs three short-video channels (Country Flux, PomProds and FaktaMantra) on YouTube, Instagram and
Facebook. This app is an internal tool. It only publishes our own videos as Reels to our own Instagram accounts and
Facebook Pages.

- **What it accesses:** only the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts owned by the Prony Production
  business portfolio, to publish videos and read back the link of each post.
- **Personal data:** the app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any user or viewer. It has no
  login for the public and does not read comments, messages or follower information.
- **Storage:** the links of our own published posts are kept in our private production spreadsheet.
- **Data deletion:** the app holds no user data. To ask a question or request removal of anything related to you,
  contact us through the "About" page of any of our channels, or open an issue in this repository.
