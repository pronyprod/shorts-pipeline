# Fakta unik content bank (Indonesian channel, original map and infographic visuals)
Created 2026-10-29. Every topic fits the format:
- an animated map or infographic (never other people's footage);
- a hook number or question in the first second;
- a twist every 5–8 seconds;
- a local comparison (e.g. Sabang–Merauke);
- a question to viewers at the end.

The topic test from breakdowns3: viewers should feel smart or surprised, never depressed, and there must be a clear payoff.

**Facts rule:** the anchors below are starting points ONLY. The fakta session must find a source for each, write it in `episodes/EpNNN.md`, and drop or rephrase anything it can't verify. Some topics are marked ⚠ because the science is disputed: present those carefully or skip them.

**Timely slot (owner, 2026-11-10):** about 1 in 5 episodes is timely: every episode number divisible by 5 (Ep010, Ep015, …).
- Tied to something in the news or a current date/event that fits this channel (Indonesia-related fakta).
- The timely fact needs at least one source **published in the last 30 days**; write its date next to it in SOURCES and `episodes/EpNNN.md`. Older sources are fine for background facts.
- **Nothing political** (elections, parties, politicians, government decisions, disputes between countries, protests, war) and **nothing tragic** (deaths, disasters with victims, accidents, crime, disease outbreaks).
- It must still be true and worth watching when it publishes 1–3 days later; skip news that is still changing.
- All the normal rules still apply (category rotation, sources, payoff, nothing depressing).
- Timely topics are researched when the episode is made, not stored ahead. When one is used, add it to the episode log with "Timely" and the source date. If nothing passes, make a normal episode and write "no timely topic found" in the log line and the report.

## New category: "Kenapa…? (cara kerja)" (added 2026-11-05)
**Why:** ID scan of 5 Nov (`results\2026-11-05_ID_kenapa\`). The query "kenapa", filtered to explainer uploads only (n=29), had a typical median of **27,862** views with **83% >10k**; new channels (n=10) had a median of 26,920 with 70% >10k. That's comparable to fakta unik (28,660 / 76% / 80%) and what-if (37,514 / 69% / 73%). The other phrasings ("cara kerja", "kenapa bisa", "proses pembuatan", "fakta sains", "how it works") were weak (typical medians 199–1,648), so **title every episode as "Kenapa …?"**, never "Cara kerja …".

**Structure (second person, build it from zero):** "Kalau kamu … , kamu sudah punya X. Tapi … (problem) → jadi kamu … (fix) → tapi … (new problem) → …" with 4–6 steps, then the payoff that answers the title question near the end. Use our own closing question and visual world: **no mascot, village diorama or closing line from How Come?**, and none of their topics (roof leak, floor tiles, railway crossing, and the others seen in their latest uploads: brick bonds, jet engine fans, Semanggi interchange, wet sacks on concrete, tripping breakers, curly instant noodles, SUTET lines, fridges after moving).
