# Countryballs "Flag Family": verified flag-pair bank
Created 2026-10-29. Every pair below was checked visually against the flag-icons SVG files in
`remotion-studio\countryballs-video\node_modules\flag-icons\flags\4x3\` (the exact flags our videos use).

**Rules**
- Parent flag → child flag where the child's flag looks like a "modified" version of the parents'.
- **Excluded on purpose:** Ep001's pairs (JP→BD, NL→LU, IT→MX, PL→ID) and GeoTurkAnimation's
  known pairs (CY→XK, RU→PL, PH→CZ, VN→MA). Never copy another channel's pairs or jokes.
- Avoided politically heated pairs (e.g. disputed territories, current conflicts).
- Punchlines: 3–6 words, deadpan, explaining the visual difference. Alternatives given; edit freely.
- Reactions come from the CLAUDE.md list (shocked, squint, disappointed, confused, proud, smug,
  defiant, innocent, laughing, crying). Never repeat a reaction in consecutive rounds.
- Codes are flag-icons codes (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, lowercase in code).
- **Timely slot (owner, 2026-11-10):** about 1 in 5 episodes is timely: every episode number divisible by 5 (Ep010, Ep015, …). At least one round features a country or flag that is in the news, or a current date (e.g. a national day this month).
  - The timely fact needs at least one source **published in the last 30 days**; note it with its date in the episode's facts. Older sources are fine for background facts.
  - **Nothing political** (elections, politicians, government decisions, disputes between countries, protests, war) and **nothing tragic** (deaths, disasters with victims, accidents, crime, disease outbreaks). The punchline stays about the flag's look.
  - It must still hold up when it publishes 1–3 days later. All the other rules here still apply.
  - If nothing passes, make a normal episode and write "no timely topic found" in the log line and the report. Mark a used timely round "Timely" with the source date.
