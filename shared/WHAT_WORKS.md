# What works (weekly review, 2026-11-21)  TEST RUN

**Data window:** episodes posted 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-16, views at 48 h (31 episodes with numbers: Country Flux 11, PomProds 10, Fakta Unik 10). 7 newer episodes had no numbers yet and are skipped (Countryballs Ep012-014, What If Ep014-015, Fakta Ep012, Ep014). Numbers come only from `ci-out/review/episodes.json`. Per-episode styles (A/B/D/P) are not in the input; they are quoted only where `shared/WHATIF_BANK.md` records them.

## Summary for the owner
- Country Flux is far ahead: median 14,774 views at 48 h and 99.8% average viewed (412 subs from 11 episodes). Fakta Unik: median 2,854 views, 72.6%. PomProds: median 1,053 views, 52.5%, 9 subs from 10 episodes.
- Flag Family stays the engine (7 episodes, median 19,513; best Ep008 31,214 and Ep002 26,952). Fakta's one breakout is Borobudur (Ep007, 19,524 views, 25 subs). PomProds reach is flat (7 of 10 episodes between 1,042 and 1,303), so topic is not the lever yet; the best on both views and retention was the 15-second countdown space episode (Ep008).
- Banks leaned: unused items re-ordered, 3 weak or repeating ones moved to the end, new ideas added in the direction of the winners (details in `shared/PROJECT_LOG.md`).

**What works**
- *Flag Family (the original format):* Ep002 26,952, Ep008 31,214, Ep007 20,865, Ep004 19,513, Ep003 14,774 (median of the 7 Ep002-008 episodes: 19,513). Twins (Ep010, 21,672) did as well on one episode: early sign.
- *Concrete visual joke in the hook and title* ("learned CHESS", "left the star at home", "white stripe went on a DIET", "planted a TREE"): Ep002, Ep008, Ep007, Ep004 are four of the top five.
- *CAPS word + emoji in the title:* CAPS episodes (Ep001, 002, 003, 004, 007, 010, 011) median 19,513 versus 12,883 without (Ep005, 006, 008, 009). Not a rule: the best episode (Ep008, 31,214) has no CAPS word, and Ep001 (6,150) and Ep011 (7,298) have one and flopped.
- *Retention:* 99.8% median; five episodes are above 100% (Ep001 104.3, Ep005 103.3, Ep011 102.4, Ep010 101.2, Ep007 100.8), i.e. looping or rewatching.

**What does not**
- *Generic or question hooks:* Ep005 ("Some kids rebel by turning the whole flag around", 2,635 views, the lowest), Ep001 ("so what did the kid change?", 6,150), Ep011 ("which one is bigger?", 7,298). Two question hooks = early sign.
- *Size duel (Ep011, 7,298):* lowest of the three new sub-formats, but one episode only: early sign. Reverse quiz Ep009 14,658 (one episode: early sign, around the channel median).
- *Reach and retention disagree:* the three highest average % viewed (Ep001 104.3, Ep005 103.3, Ep011 102.4) are three of the four lowest reach. Retention is saturated; reach is decided by topic, title and hook.
- Subs follow reach: Ep002 99, Ep008 58, Ep007 56, Ep004 56 (269 of 412).

**Next 2 weeks**
1. Keep Flag Family as the core: next two Flag Family episodes use the spare pairs plus the new ones (see the bank order).
2. Hook = the concrete change in plain words ("One lost a star, another traded the sun for a bird"), never "which one is...?".
3. Title = one visual noun in CAPS + an emoji + "Guess the ..." (the Ep002/Ep007/Ep010 pattern).
4. Do the size duel last, and give it a hook that names the stake and a CAPS word; judge it again after Ep019's numbers.
5. Twins: add a second batch at the next review if Ep018's numbers hold.

**What works**
- *Countdown threat in line 1:* Ep008 ("about 15 seconds in space", Deadly place) is the best episode on both views (1,303) and avg % viewed (64.1%). One episode: early sign.
- *Absurd scale with a huge number:* Ep013 ants 1,218, Ep009 $1 trillion 1,064 (2 episodes, both above the median; early sign). Ep005 rope paradox 1,210 and Ep003 jumps 1,239 are in the same range.
- *Reach is flat:* 7 of 10 episodes are between 1,042 and 1,303 views whatever the round (round 1-2 maths: Ep001, 003, 005; round 3: Ep008, 009, 013). The pivot to "you + danger" did not move median reach (Ep001-005 median 1,042; Ep008/009/011/012/013 median 1,064).

**What does not**
- *Food/drink:* Ep011 coffee, 272 views (but see the retention note). *Real survivor:* Ep012 whale, 406. *Journey:* Ep004 tunnel, 99 (the lowest). One episode each: early sign only.
- *"What if you ..." title is not the difference:* with "you" (Ep003, 004, 005, 008, 011, 012, 013) median 1,210 views and 53.1%; without (Ep001, 002, 009) median 1,042 and 51.7%.
- *Ep002 dominoes 684, Ep012 whale 406:* hooks over 25 words (Ep012) did not help, but Ep013 (long hook) got 1,218: no hook-length pattern.

**Reach and retention disagree:** Ep011 (coffee) has the best avg % viewed of the channel (120.2%, avg view 66 s) and the second-lowest reach (272 views, 0 to 1 sub). Ep008 is the only one that is good on both.

**Next 2 weeks**
1. Open on a countdown or hard time limit in line 1 (Ep008 pattern); prefer Deadly place and Absurd scale (bank: #29, #32, #36, #37, #38).
2. Don't run Food/drink or Real survivor again before 2 more episodes of other categories are in (#30 and #34 moved to the end of the order). Early sign only, one episode each.
3. Check what Ep011's ending did to reach 120.2% (a loop back to line 1?) and try the same ending on a different premise; keep the hook strong, because that episode's reach was low.
4. Because reach is flat, test packaging on one episode: a CAPS word in the title (the Country Flux pattern). Treat as a test, not a finding.
5. Keep the timely slot (Ep020, Ep025); no numbers for Ep015 yet.

**What works**
- *Borobudur (Ep007):* 19,524 views, 84.3%, 25 subs, 66 s average view: nearly 4x the next episode and 35 of the channel's 54 subs together with Ep001 (10). Hook corrects a belief ("warga sudah tahu duluan"). One episode: early sign.
- *Indonesia compared with a big country or the world* (Ep001 vs USA 4,968 / 86.9%; Ep011 one island, three countries 4,864; Ep007 biggest Buddha temple): three of the top four.
- *Timely (Ep010 Leonid, 3,602, 73.2%)* is above the median: early sign (one episode). Kenapa (Ep005, 3,449, 73.6%) also above it: early sign.

**What does not**
- *Animals and plain science facts:* Ep009 Komodo 2,008 / 40.5%, Ep008 no-shadow day 1,520 / 42.5%: both the lowest retention. Ep004 Manhattan (history) 1,310 / 50.8% was also weak, so history alone is not the cause (Ep007 and Ep003 are history too: 19,524 and 2,259).
- *Reach and retention disagree:* Ep003 Krakatau has 90.2% but only 2,259 views and 1 sub; Ep011 has 4,864 views at 72%. Retention alone does not drive reach.
- Title formula is not a driver: numbers (Ep002 1,911; Ep003 2,259; Ep010 3,602) and superlatives (Ep007 19,524; Ep009 2,008) land on both sides.

**Next 2 weeks**
1. More "Indonesia vs the world" and heritage topics (bank: V10, V12, V11 first).
2. Reuse the Ep007 hook type: start from what people think they know, then correct it.
3. Fewer animal/science one-fact episodes (Ep008, Ep009); if used, add a comparison to a country or object people know.
4. Keep Ep020 and Ep025 as timely slots; Ep010's result is encouraging.
5. Keep one Kenapa per ~5 episodes (Ep005 above the median); lean on the fact-style ones, not how-it-works, until one has numbers.
