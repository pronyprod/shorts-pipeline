**What works**
- **Reach is the variable, retention is not.** Avg % viewed is 93-104% on all 11 (the video loops), so every episode is watched to the end; views separate them (2,635 to 31,214).
- **Classic Flag Family** (8 episodes: Ep001-008): 6 of 8 are above 11,000 views. Best: Ep008 (31,214), Ep002 (26,952), Ep007 (20,865), Ep004 (19,513). Subs follow: Ep008 58, Ep007 56, Ep004 56.
- **Titles of the form "This kid / Their kid + a visual verb + CAPS word + emoji + Guess the flag"** are behind all four top classics (Ep008, 002, 007, 004). Concrete visual "action" words (planted a TREE, learned CHESS, DIET, left the star at home) beat vague ones.
- **Twins** (Ep010, 21,672 views, 32 subs): 1 episode, an early sign that it can match the classics.
- **Reverse quiz** (Ep009, 14,658, 49 subs): 1 episode, an early sign of a solid format (about the median).

**What does not**
- **Size duel** (Ep011, 7,298 views, 9 subs): 1 episode, an early sign it reaches about half the median; its title is a claim about geography ("SMALLER than Algeria?!") rather than a kid on a flag.
- **Ep005 "flipped the whole flag" (2,635, 3 subs)** and **Ep001 "TOUCHED GRASS" (6,150)**: the two weakest. Ep005's title says "Can you guess?" instead of naming what to guess ("Guess the flag / parents"); Ep001's meme phrase doesn't describe a flag change. Early signs (2 episodes).
- Words (Ep012) and later formats have no numbers yet.
- **Length:** every episode is 18-22 s average view; no length signal.

**Next 2 weeks**
1. Keep the classic Flag Family as the base (about every second episode), using the new unused pairs in `shared/FLAG_PAIR_BANK.md` (#33-#37).
2. Title formula: "[This/Their kid] + [concrete visual verb] + CAPS word + one emoji + Guess the flag/parents". Avoid memes and avoid "Can you guess?" with no object.
3. Twins and Reverse quiz stay in the rotation (1 episode each, both strong); Size duel only 1 in about 5 until a second data point exists.
4. Watch the Ep012-Ep019 numbers next week (Words, Reverse, Twins, Size duel round 2) before cutting any format.

**What works**
- **Retention:** Ep008 (64.1%, "stepped into space without a suit": hook is a countdown to "you", "You get about 15 seconds…") is the best normal retention. Ep011 shows 120.2% and a 66 s average view, which means it loops, but on only 272 views (reach vs retention disagree; early sign for style P).
- **Reach:** the top five (Ep008, 003, 013, 005, 009; 1,064-1,303 views) are all concrete, big-number premises. After Round 3 (Ep008, 009, 011, 012, 013), "Absurd scale" (Ep009 1,064; Ep013 1,218) and "Deadly place" (Ep008 1,303) are the steadier categories. 2 episodes each: early signs.

**What does not**
- **Whale / Real survivor, style D** (Ep012: 406 views, 49.5%) and **Food/drink style P** (Ep011: 272 views): the two Round 3 episodes in D and P reached the fewest viewers; 1 episode each, so early signs, and a style effect cannot be separated from topic here.
- **Journey (Ep004, 99 views)** is the weakest by far; "What if you jumped into a tunnel…" has no danger or number in the title. 1 episode.
- **The Round 3 pivot has not clearly helped yet:** Ep001-005 (growth/journey/paradox) median 1,042 views and 51.9%; Ep008-013 (Round 3) median 1,064 views and 54.8%. About the same reach, a small retention rise. Only 5 vs 5 episodes.
- Average view is 25-43 s on every normal episode (below the 40-60 s target length), so people leave before the end.

**Next 2 weeks**
1. Lean on Deadly place and Absurd scale (B, or D when it is a journey) for the normal premises; keep "you" + a number in the first line (the Ep008 hook).
2. Put the first number or danger in the title ("…without a suit", "…$1 trillion") and drop vague titles.
3. Test PomProds Stories (style D) as planned (about 2 a week), then compare their views with Ep012's 406; do not decide on D until Ep016 and the Stories have numbers.
4. Cut every episode to land at 40-60 s; the midpoint drop is the common problem (average view 25-35 s on 7 of 10 episodes).

- **Length:** the best episodes have 58-66 s average view (Ep007, Ep001); the three weakest retentions (Ep009, Ep008, Ep004) all have 33 s or less.

**Next 2 weeks**
1. More history and geography topics with a famous local place and a "hidden / bigger than you think" twist (new V11-V14 in `shared/FAKTA_BANK.md`).
2. Keep Kenapa topics to about 2 of the next 14 (Ep005 was above the median; nothing more to go on).
3. For the 2-part series use Krakatau + Tambora (science and size, no victim counts), not the Rempah/Manhattan one (Ep004 was weak).
4. No second-person opening for the next 3 episodes (Ep008 left early); keep the number-first hook.
5. Skip animals for now until Ep012 has numbers.
