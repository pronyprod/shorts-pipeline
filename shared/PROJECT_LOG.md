## 3. Platform and monetization facts (researched 2026-10-29)

### YouTube Partner Program (YPP)
- **From 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants):** 1,000 subscribers + **8,000** long-form watch hours in 365 days **or** **20M** Shorts views in 90 days. That's double the current 4,000 hours / 10M. Announced 10 Aug 2026. (ppc.land, NewscastStudio)
- Existing YPP members keep their status, but from 1 Feb 2027 they need **10M Shorts views per rolling 90 days** to keep earning Shorts revenue. Modules must be accepted by 31 Jan 2027.
- **Lower tier, NOT changing:** 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + (3,000 watch hours in 12 months **or 3M Shorts views in 90 days**). It unlocks fan funding and **YouTube Shopping affiliate**. (YouTube Help)
- **YouTube Shopping affiliate:** Indonesia is eligible since 27 Mar 2026, at 500 subscribers within YPP. Products are linked through Shopee. **Excludes "Made for Kids" and music channels.**
- **Inauthentic content policy** (renamed July 2025): AI and templates are allowed. Near-identical, mass-produced, template videos with no original contribution get demonetized. Channels are judged as a whole. Protection: real variety, commentary or value, editing, your own voice. (AuditSocials, MILX)
- **AI disclosure label:** not needed for animation, unrealistic visuals, or AI-assisted scripts. Needed for realistic synthetic people or events and for cloned real voices. The label does not reduce earnings.
- **Shorts ad pay:** typically **$0.07–0.20 per 1,000 views**, 3–14% of long-form RPM (AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels). US-audience Shorts RPM is about $0.33.
- **Indonesia vs US ad rates:** long-form RPM about **$0.41 (ID) vs $10.81 (US)** (Lenostube estimate). Pay depends on **viewer** location, not creator location.
- **Made for Kids:** non-personalised ads only (CPM about $1–3 for nursery rhymes and animation), no comments, no bell notifications, no end screens, no Shopping. YouTube has demonetized low-quality kids channels. Google faced pressure in April 2026 over AI videos for kids.

### Other platforms
- **TikTok Creator Rewards: NOT available in Indonesia.** As of July 2026 only US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR, MX. (Quasa)
- **TikTok Shop affiliate (yellow cart):** about 600 followers, 18+, KTP verification, 5–10% commission set by the seller. (Dealls, Detik)
- **Shopee Affiliate:** no follower minimum, up to 4% commission (more on "Komisi XTRA" products), paid to ShopeePay under Rp500k and by bank transfer above. (Dealls)
- **Facebook Content Monetization:** available in Indonesia (about 1.7M Indonesian monetized accounts, the largest non-English group). Requirements per a third-party guide: 10k Page followers + 600k minutes viewed in 60 days + 5 videos. Reposts with watermarks get demonetized. (Rest of World, ShortSync)
- **Instagram:** no reliable Reels payout program in Indonesia.

### Copyright notes
- Classic Indonesian *lagu anak* by known composers (Ibu Sud, Pak Kasur, A.T. Mahmud...) are mostly still under copyright: Indonesian law protects songs for the composer's life + 70 years. Old English nursery rhymes are public domain.
- Never use copyrighted characters (Upin Ipin, Doraemon, Cocomelon...) or trending copyrighted music.

### 6d. Voice research (2026-10-29 09:05)
