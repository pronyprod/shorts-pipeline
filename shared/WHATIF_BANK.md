# "What if" content bank (English channel)
Created 2026-10-29. Each premise follows the format rules from breakdowns2 and breakdowns3:
- one concrete absurd premise, shown in frame 1;
- a counter that keeps CLIMBING (plus a progress bar);
- a ladder of comparisons to familiar things;
- a climax, then a twist;
- a "what should we try next?" call to action;
- topics that leave viewers feeling smart, never depressing.

**Variety rule** (YouTube's Oct 2026 originality update): vary the *mechanic*, not only the object. Mechanics used here are growth, height, speed, loudness, a chain reaction, spin and heat.

**Facts rule:** the "anchors" below are starting points. The production session must compute every number in code, source every comparison (CLAUDE.md rule), and flag anything uncertain.

**Timely slot (owner, 2026-11-10):** about 1 in 5 episodes is timely: every episode number divisible by 5 (Ep010, Ep015, …).
- A "What if you…?" premise tied to something in the news that fits the channel (science, space, nature, records, weird true events).
- The timely fact needs at least one source **published in the last 30 days**; write its date in the `FACTS` block. Older sources are fine for background facts.
- **Nothing political** (elections, politicians, government decisions, disputes between countries, protests, war) and **nothing tragic** (deaths, disasters with victims, accidents, crime, disease outbreaks). Round 3's monetisation guardrails still apply in full.
- It must still hold up when it publishes 1–3 days later. Category rotation still applies.
- Researched when the episode is made, not stored ahead. When used, add it to "Used in Round 3" with "Timely" and the source date. If nothing passes, take the next normal premise and write "no timely topic found" in the log line and the report.

**Done elsewhere (owner, 2026-11-10):** before producing a premise, search YouTube for it. If a big channel has done it (roughly 100K+ subscribers, or a 1M+ view Short on the same premise), **don't skip it**: note it in the bank row ("Done by <channel>, <views>, <date>") and make ours clearly different, with a different angle, a different twist and our own wording. Never copy their script, visuals, jokes or title. Check only the title, description and captions, just enough to know what to avoid.

## Round 3: owner pivot (added 2026-11-09) — USE THIS ROUND ONLY
Owner feedback, 9 Nov: the math/physics puzzles are boring and viewers leave early (Ep001 kept 57.9% avg viewed vs 80%+ on the
other channels). New direction: **"you" + danger or an absurd amount + a real fact or real person as the payoff.** The viewer
must picture it happening to THEM in the first 2 seconds. Rounds 1-2 are retired: don't use their unused items.

**Categories** (replace "shapes"; never the same category twice in a row): Body limit, Food/drink, Absurd scale,
Deadly place, Real survivor.

**Style rules (override CLAUDE.md where they conflict):**
- Line 1 = the most extreme moment, said to "you", no intro, no "imagine": e.g. "You have 15 seconds before you black out."
- "You" in nearly every line. Escalate every 3-5 s; each beat is worse or bigger than the last.
- Keep the climbing counter (seconds, metres deep, cups, strikes, tonnes): it fits the locked template.
- The twist is a REAL fact or a REAL person it happened to ("This actually happened to ... in 1965"). Source it.
- Length 40-60 s (owner, 12 Nov). Write to about 2.3 words per second (about 90-135 words). The voice is never sped up past
  1.05x: too long = cut words. Voice: clear, unhurried, punch on the key line.
- Title: "What if you ...?" under 50 characters, and it must be true to the video.
- Ending: "What should you survive next? The best comment becomes the next video."

**Monetisation guardrails (so the channel stays ad-friendly and eligible for the Partner Program):**
- No drugs, alcohol, vaping, weapons, self-harm or suicide, sexual content. Use caffeine, sugar, chilli, water instead.
- Cartoon only: no blood, gore, wounds or bodies. Say "you'd black out" / "your body gives up", never graphic death.
- Never frame anything as a challenge to try. Show consequences; nothing a kid could copy at home step by step.
- No fake "this really happened": every real case needs a source. No misleading titles or thumbnails.
- Original script every time, and vary the category (YouTube demonetises repetitive, template-looking channels).

**Best style (owner, 2026-11-11; revised 2026-11-12: the topic picks the style, not the calendar):** every Round 3 idea
has the style that fits it best and a second choice, e.g. "D (B)". B = blocky 3D (fits almost anything), D = paper cut-out
with one continuous camera (stories, real survivors, journeys), A = flat (big numbers and side-by-side comparisons),
P = pixel (inside your body, tech, games). Judged on fit alone (re-checked 12 Nov); the soft target of about 1 D and 1 A-or-P
a week is handled when an episode is made (whatif-video `.claude/commands/next-episode.md`). Every new idea gets one too.
